    Roadmap to the platinum

    55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. No online trophies — all 55 are single-player (KeenGamer). Expect around 200 hours (KeenGamer). There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (OutputLag).

    Missable: exactly one trophy is flagged as potentially missable by the launch-era guides — Positive Mental Attitude. Lucia's balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter (OutputLag). Meditate there during Chapter 1.

      Sources & notes

      How to read this guide

      • Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list as published on PSNProfiles and Exophase, cross-checked by PlanetGTA6 — the two lists agree on all 55.
      • Every guide is written for this entry in our own words and cites its sources beside it. A specific location or step with no source gets no credit from us either: where launch-era guides had not confirmed a method, the guide says so instead of inventing one.
      • 28 of the 55 trophies are hidden on PlayStation until earned. With spoilers hidden, story trophies show as “Hidden story trophy” until you tick them earned.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx, via PlanetGTA6) — do not enable cheats on a platinum run.
      • Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your machine except through Export, which downloads a file you can import back.
